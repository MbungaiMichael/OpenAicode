# **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

Product: International Patient Assistance Platform

## **1\. Product Overview**

A digital platform designed to help international patients, especially those from African countries, access medical care in India by providing hospital information, translation services, treatment coordination, and assistance throughout their healthcare journey.

## **2\. Target Users**

* International patients traveling to India for medical treatment.  
* Patients from African countries who face language and cultural barriers.  
* Family members or caregivers accompanying patients.  
* Translators and patient coordinators assisting international patients.

## **3\. Problem Statement**

International patients traveling to India often struggle with language barriers, navigating unfamiliar hospitals, understanding medical procedures, estimating treatment costs, and managing payments.

The platform aims to simplify their healthcare journey by providing accessible information and connecting them with the right support services.

## **4\. Product Goals**

* Simplify access to hospitals and healthcare services in India.  
* Connect patients with translators and patient coordinators.  
* Improve patients' understanding of treatment information and estimated costs.  
* Help patients navigate hospitals and manage their medical journey.  
* Provide a centralized platform for international patient assistance.

## **6\. Main User Journey**

1\. Register or sign in

Create an account and select preferred language.

2\. Find healthcare services

Search hospitals, specialties, and medical services.

3\. Request assistance

Connect with a translator or patient coordinator.

4\. Plan medical treatment

Request appointments, understand treatment information, and receive cost estimates.

5\. Receive on-ground support

Navigate hospital facilities and receive assistance during hospital visits.

6\. Complete the healthcare journey

Access follow-up support and relevant medical documents.

## **7\. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**

The first version should include:

* Patient registration and profile creation.  
* Hospital and medical service directory.  
* Translator and patient coordinator contact or request form.  
* Appointment assistance request.  
* Treatment cost estimate request.  
* Basic hospital location and navigation information.  
* Patient support contact feature.

## **8\. Success Metrics**

* Number of registered international patients.  
* Number of hospital assistance requests submitted.  
* Number of successful translator and coordinator connections.  
* Number of appointment requests completed.  
* Patient satisfaction ratings.

## **9\. Product Scope and Limitations**

The platform will help patients access information and coordinate healthcare services. It will not independently diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatments, or guarantee treatment costs or hospital availability. Medical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

Expected outcome: A centralized, accessible platform that helps international patients navigate healthcare services in India with greater clarity, coordination, and support.
